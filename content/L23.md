A linear policy $u=u_\mathrm{eq}-K(x-x_\mathrm{eq})$. CEM samples the 12 gains, keeps the elite (highest scores), repeats.

Reward is $-\mathrm{cost}$. Lab 04's LQR is the dashed line labelled "zero samples." You will not beat it honestly. At $dt=0.05$ that $K$ bang-bangs (fast attitude pole); $dt=0.02$ is the honest comparison. A near-zero $K$ on a short horizon is a reward hack.
