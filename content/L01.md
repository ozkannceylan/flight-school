A robot is three things: a state vector, a vector field $\dot x=f(x,u)$, and a clock.

Euler is $x \leftarrow x + dt\,f$. Local error $O(dt^2)$, global $O(dt)$. RK4 evaluates $f$ four times and cancels the next three error terms — global $O(dt^4)$. On a conservative system the difference is visible as energy drift.

Lab 00 asks you to implement both and drop a point mass. The picture — two trajectories leaving each other — is the whole course: the same $f$, a worse clock, a different life.
