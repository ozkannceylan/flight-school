Grids die in high dimension. RRT grows a tree toward random samples; the only primitive is steer-and-collide.

RRT* rewires for asymptotic optimality. Shortcutting is the cheap post-process everyone remembers. Success rate over seeds, not a single pretty tree, is the check.
