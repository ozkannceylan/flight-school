Planar quadrotor state $x=[p_y,p_z,\theta,v_y,v_z,\omega]$. Inputs $u=[u_1,u_2]$ are rotor thrusts.

$$
\ddot y = -\frac{u_1+u_2}{m}\sin\theta,\quad
\ddot z = \frac{u_1+u_2}{m}\cos\theta - g,\quad
\ddot\theta = \frac{L(u_1-u_2)}{I}.
$$

Hover: $\theta=0$, $u_1=u_2=mg/2$. Two inputs, six states — underactuated. Lateral motion is purchased with tilt. This $f$ is the spine plant for the next 22 labs.
