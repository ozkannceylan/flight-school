Nonuniform terrain: Dijkstra is BFS with a cost-aware frontier (heap).

$A^*$ adds $h$. Admissible $\Rightarrow$ $A^*$ cost $=$ Dijkstra cost, fewer expansions. Inflate $h$ by $\times 2$ and optimality breaks on purpose. Manhattan is admissible on 4-connected grids; Euclidean is too, and usually expands less.
