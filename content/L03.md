3D quadrotor: 12 states. ZYX Euler is fine until pitch hits $\pm 90^\circ$ and two axes coincide — gimbal lock. The representation, not the physics, blows up.

A proportional altitude loop is the first closed loop: $u_\Sigma = mg + k_p(z_d-z)$. It does not care about yaw. Drive the representation through the singularity on purpose so you see the difference between "the robot failed" and "the chart failed."
