Mapping is localization inverted: pose known, map unknown.

Inverse beam: cells along a hit ray go free, the hit cell goes occupied. Log-odds $l=\log p/(1-p)$ turns multiply-and-underflow into add. A naive $p\leftarrow p\cdot \eta$ implementation underflows on purpose so you see why the log exists.
