Eight noisy samples of $\sin 2x$, a degree-8 polynomial. Interpolation is memorization.

$w=(\Phi^\top\Phi+\lambda I)^{-1}\Phi^\top y$ with $I_{00}=0$ (do not penalise the intercept). Gap $(v-t)/(v+t)$ is large with $\lambda=0$ and drops under 0.18 with a modest $\lambda$. Fitting better on the train set can mean knowing less.
