One hidden layer: $h=\mathrm{relu}(xW_1+b_1)$, $\hat y=hW_2+b_2$. Loss is mean MSE over every entry, so $\partial L/\partial\hat y=2(\hat y-y)/\hat y.\mathrm{size}$.

Backprop is the chain rule plus the ReLU mask $z>0$. Finite differences at $10^{-5}$ are the check, not a vibe. Then close the loop on Lab 04's dataset: a matrix you fitted, flying.
