Graph search is a frontier. BFS on unit costs is shortest-path optimal; DFS is not, and can wander.

The shared interface is `pop` / `push`. Occupancy grid: 4-connected, blocked cells are not nodes. Expansion counts are the honest cost — path length alone hides the work.
