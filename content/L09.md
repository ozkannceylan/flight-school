A quadrotor is differentially flat in $(y,z)$ (planar) / $(x,y,z,\psi)$ (3D). Plan the flat outputs with min-snap polynomials; recover $\theta$ and $u$ algebraically.

Feedforward from the flat map plus Lab 04 feedback on the tracking error is the composition test for the whole architecture. If `get("lab04")` is unfinished you still fly — the banner is the point.
