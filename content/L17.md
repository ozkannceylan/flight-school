Pinhole: $X_c=R(X-C)$, $u=f_x X_c/Z_c+c_x$, $v=f_y Y_c/Z_c+c_y$.

$K$ is four numbers. Given known pose and correspondences they are two 2-parameter least-squares problems (`xn=X_c/Z_c`). Inverse projection is a ray — depth is free. That ambiguity is why Lab 17 needs a ground plane.
