Linearize $f$ about hover. Closed-loop eigenvalues in the LHP are the stability verdict.

Cascade: inner PD on $\theta$ (fast), outer PD on $(y,z)$ that *requests* a $\theta_d$ (slow). Timescale separation $\ge 5$ is the thing you tune, not a single gain. A 3×3 gain-sweep grid is how you feel the cliff between sluggish and unstable.
