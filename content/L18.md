Brightness constancy: $I_x u + I_y v + I_t=0$. LK solves that in a window.

A downward camera: $u_\mathrm{pix}\approx -f_x v_y/z$. Invert the mean $u$ and you have a rate. On `blank`, $G$ is singular and LK is noise — the aperture problem, not a bug. A hover PD that treats that noise as $\dot y$ is Lab 23's adversarial test.
