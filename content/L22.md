A 3×3 conv is a structural prior: the same edge detector at every offset.

On 24×24 synthetic gates, a 4-filter conv plus an argmax-over-width readout beats a parameter-matched flatten-MLP, *if* you hold out the right-hand columns. Global average pooling over $H,W$ destroys location. NumPy is the default; `BACKEND=torch` is optional.
