KF: Gaussian closed form on the hover linearization. PF: samples on the full nonlinear $f$.

NEES asks whether the KF's covariance is honest. Break the KF with a nonlinearity it did not linearise (large $\theta$). The PF degrades differently — impoverishment, not linearization. Same data, two ways to be wrong.
