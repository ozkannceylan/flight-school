A geometric path is not a trajectory until it has a clock. Scale time by $s$: derivatives pick up $1/s, 1/s^2, \ldots$

Peak rotor thrust is monotonic in how fast you fly the same spline. The smallest $s$ that keeps $u$ inside the box is a one-dimensional search. Time is the free variable; the motors are not.
