Infinite-horizon LQR: minimise $\int (x^\top Qx + u^\top Ru)\,dt$. The CARE $A^\top P+PA-PBR^{-1}B^\top P+Q=0$ gives $K=R^{-1}B^\top P$.

$Q$ and $R$ are a design language, not a moral ranking. Fat $R$ saturates actuators; skinny $R$ bangs. Optimal $\neq$ intuitive. Race it against your Lab 03 PD on the same nudge — that gap is the lesson Lab 22 will spend samples failing to close.
