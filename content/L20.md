A skinny bowl $(w_0-1)^2+25(w_1+0.5)^2$. SGD zigzags; momentum overshoots; Adam rescales the axes.

The deliverable is the cliff: the smallest `lr` whose SGD loss exceeds 80 or goes non-finite. Start at the origin — some starts are already over the line. How you step is a different lesson from what the gradient is.
