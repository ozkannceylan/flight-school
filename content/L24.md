The laws that matter here are not Asimov's. They are the assumptions you already shipped.

- A normalised belief is not a true pose (`labs/lab11_bayes/check.py`, inverted likelihood).
- Brightness change is not velocity on a blank patch (`labs/lab17_optical_flow/check.py`).
- Train accuracy is not the world (`labs/lab21_cnn/check.py`, held-out gates).
- A cheap episode return is not a hover (`labs/lab22_rl/README.md`).

Economy: every lab bought progress by assuming something false at the boundary. Ethics / liability: a confidently wrong filter, deployed, is a product decision. The durable form of a refusal is a test — `tests/test_lab23_assumption.py` — that stays red against the old assumption and green against the mitigation. That is the law you write for your own stack.
